The short version

Pre-orders run 23 Sep to 31 Oct. We make it in one run after they close and ship by 15 Dec. Prepaid only while pre-orders are open. After launch, every order is at your door in 7–10 days. Shipping is always free in India.

01Pre-orders

Pre-order @ ₹199 per tee until 31 Oct. This is a deposit towards the full tee price, not its total price. Orders open 1 Nov. Pay your remaining balance from launch, before dispatch. Decide not to pay it? Your ₹199 stays as store credit, with no expiry, for any future order. A paid pre-order keeps its saved price for only the selected tees, sizes and quantities. Shipping is free.

02Where we ship

Anywhere in India we can get a courier to. That's most pin codes. If yours isn't covered, checkout will tell you before you pay anything.

We don't ship outside India yet.

03What it costs

Nothing. Shipping is free on every order, whatever the size of it.

04How long it takes

Pre-order
You order. It's at your door in 7–10 days.

That's calendar days, counted from the day you order to the day it arrives, weekends included. The exact arrival window is shown on the product page and in your bag before you pay.

05Cash on delivery

Not available for pre-orders, which are prepaid. After launch, it's available on orders across India. There's no extra charge for it. Please keep the exact amount ready. Couriers rarely have change.

If a COD order is refused at the door without a reason, we may ask for prepayment on your next order.

06Tracking

When your order leaves us, you'll get an email and SMS with a tracking link. That link is the tracker. More on the track order page.

07Delays and lost parcels

Couriers occasionally lose their way. If your order hasn't moved for a few days, or tracking says delivered and it wasn't, write to abhijeet@wearmudra.shop with your order number. We'll chase it. If it's actually lost, we reprint and resend it at no cost, or refund you. Your call.

08Wrong address

Check it at checkout. If you spot a mistake, email us straight away. We can fix it any time before dispatch. After that it's up to the courier.
