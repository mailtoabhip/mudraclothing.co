The short version

We dispatch in 1–2 days and it's at your door in 5–7 days in most of India; remote pin codes can take longer. Shipping is always free, cash on delivery is available on every order, and size exchanges are free within 7 days.

02Where we ship

Anywhere in India we can get a courier to. That's most pin codes. If yours isn't covered, checkout will tell you before you pay anything.

We ship within India only.

03What it costs

Nothing. Shipping is free on every order, whatever the size of it.

04How long it takes

We pack and hand over to the courier within 1–2 days of your order.
Most of India

Remote pin codes

That's calendar days, counted from the day you order to the day it arrives, weekends included. We'll email you when your tee ships.

05Cash on delivery

Available on every order across India. There's no extra charge for it. Please keep the exact amount ready. Couriers rarely have change.

If a COD order is refused at the door without a reason, we may ask for prepayment on your next order.

06Size exchanges

Wrong size? We'll swap it for a different size of the same tee, free, within 7 days of delivery, as long as that size is available. Start it the same way as a return, on the returns page.

07Tracking

When your order leaves us, you'll get an email and SMS with a tracking link. That link is the tracker. More on the track order page.

08Delays and lost parcels

Couriers occasionally lose their way. If your order hasn't moved for a few days, or tracking says delivered and it wasn't, write to abhijeet@wearmudra.shop with your order number. We'll chase it. If it's actually lost, we reprint and resend it at no cost, or refund you. Your call.

09Wrong address

Check it at checkout. If you spot a mistake, email us straight away. We can fix it any time before dispatch. After that it's up to the courier.
