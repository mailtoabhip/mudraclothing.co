Company · About

We make
t-shirts.

Mudra Studios is a small Indian streetwear label. We design oversized tees with a quiet front and a loud back, and release them in drops.

Every design starts with something we already love: a plate of chaat, a number plate, a crashed Windows dialog, a tarot card that reads you too well. We draw it flat, print it big, and put it on the back where it belongs.

The front gets a small stamp and nothing else. You decide when to turn around.

It's run by one designer, who also builds the website and answers the emails. Nothing is made until you order it, so there's no warehouse of unsold stock and no end-of-season bonfire.

The rest is between you and your mirror.
