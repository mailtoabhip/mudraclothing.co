Chest is measured all the way round. To compare with a tee you own, lay it flat, measure armpit to armpit and double it. Supplier measurements, ±1 in. We re-check them on our own samples.

HOW TO MEASURE

The quickest way to pick a size: take a tee you already like the fit of, lay it flat, and compare.

  1. A · ChestStraight across, armpit to armpit, then double it. The chart gives the chest all the way round.
  2. B · LengthFrom the highest point of the shoulder, straight down to the hem.
  3. C · ShoulderSeam to seam across the back. On a drop shoulder, the seam sits past your actual shoulder.
  4. D · SleeveFrom the shoulder seam to the sleeve hem.
Fit

Unisex, boxy and relaxed through the body. The shoulder seam sits down the arm on purpose. Take your usual size, or one down for a closer fit.
