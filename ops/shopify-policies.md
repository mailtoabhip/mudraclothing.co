# Refund policy  (from https://www.wearmudra.shop/returns)

The short version: Changed your mind? Send it back within 7 working days of delivery. We pick it up for free. Cancel any time before it ships for a full refund.

## Returns

You can return any tee within 7 working days of delivery. No reason needed, though we're curious.

To be accepted, it needs to come back the way it arrived:

- Unworn, unwashed, and without stains, perfume or deodorant marks
- In its original packaging, if you still have it

We check every return when it reaches us. If it's clearly been worn or washed, we'll send it back to you instead of refunding. We'll email you photos first.

## Return pickup is free

You don't pay for return shipping. Once your return is approved, we book a pickup from the delivery address. If your pin code isn't covered for pickup, we'll tell you what to do instead, and still cover the cost.

## Size exchanges

Wrong size? We'll swap it for a different size of the same tee, free, within the same 7 working days, as long as that size is available. Ask for an exchange instead of a return when you write in.

## How to start a return

- Email abhijeet@wearmudra.shop within 7 working days of delivery.
- Include your order number, the tee and size, and whether you want a refund or an exchange.
- We confirm and book a pickup, usually within 2 working days.
- Hand it over. Keep the pickup receipt until your refund lands.

## Refunds

Refunds start once the return reaches us and passes the check above.

- Prepaid orders: Back to the original payment method (card, UPI, net banking or wallet) within 5–7 working days of approval. Your bank may take a few days more to show it.
- Cash on delivery: To your bank account or UPI ID, which we'll ask for by email, within 5–7 working days of approval.
- Shipping: Shipping is free, so there's nothing to deduct.

## Cancellations

You can cancel an order any time before it's dispatched, even if it's already being printed. Email us with your order number and we'll cancel it.

- Prepaid orders are refunded in full to the original payment method within 5–7 working days.
- Cash on delivery orders are simply cancelled. Nothing to refund.

Once an order has been dispatched it can't be cancelled, but you can refuse it or return it under the policy above.

We may cancel an order ourselves if a size runs out, a payment looks wrong, or we can't deliver to the address. If that happens, you get a full refund and an email explaining why.

## Pre-order deposits

The ₹199 deposit holds a tee until orders open. It isn't refundable as cash. If you decide not to buy, it stays on your Mudra account as store credit, with no expiry, and comes off any future order at checkout.

One exception: if we can't make or deliver your tee, you get the full ₹199 back, to the original payment method.

Deposits paid before 12 October 2026 keep the terms they were paid under: refundable before dispatch.

## Damaged, defective or wrong item

If something arrives damaged, misprinted or isn't what you ordered, email us within 48 hours of delivery with a photo. We'll replace it or refund it in full, and pick up the wrong one at our cost.

## Questions

Payment failed, or money debited and no order? That's on the payment help (https://www.wearmudra.shop/payment-help) page.

Anything else, write to abhijeet@wearmudra.shop. A human reads every one.

Anywhere in India we can get a courier to. That's most pin codes. If yours isn't covered, checkout will tell you before you pay anything.

Nothing. Shipping is free on every order, whatever the size of it.

Couriers occasionally lose their way. If your order hasn't moved for a few days, or tracking says delivered and it wasn't, write to abhijeet@wearmudra.shop with your order number. We'll chase it. If it's actually lost, we reprint and resend it at no cost, or refund you. Your call.

## Wrong address

Check it at checkout. If you spot a mistake, email us straight away. We can fix it any time before dispatch. After that it's up to the courier.
