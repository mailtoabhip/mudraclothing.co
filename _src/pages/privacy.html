What we collect

When you order
Your name, delivery address, phone number, email address and what you bought.
When you pay
Payment is handled by Razorpay. We see whether it went through and the method used, never your full card number or UPI PIN.
When you write to us
Your email and whatever you put in it.
When you sign up for drops
Your email address.
When you browse
Basic technical data your browser sends to any website, such as IP address, device and browser type. We don't build a profile of you from it.

Why we use it

  • To take, print, ship and deliver your order, and handle returns and refunds
  • To send order confirmations, shipping updates and replies to your emails
  • To tell you about new drops, only if you signed up for that, and you can stop any time
  • To prevent fraud and meet our legal and tax obligations

Who we share it with

Only the companies we need to get your order to you, and only what each one needs:

Shopify
Runs our checkout and stores order records.
Razorpay
Processes payments.
Print & delivery partners

Email provider

Cookies and analytics

The site uses a small amount of browser storage to remember what's in your bag. Checkout, run by Shopify, sets the cookies it needs to work. Our fonts load from Google Fonts, which sees your IP address when they load.

We don't use advertising or tracking pixels. If that ever changes, this page changes first.

How long we keep it

Order records are kept for as long as tax and accounting law requires, currently up to 8 years. Email sign-ups are kept until you unsubscribe. Emails you send us are kept while they're useful for helping you.

Keeping it safe

We use established providers for checkout and payment, keep access to order data to the people who need it, and never ask for your card details by email or phone.
