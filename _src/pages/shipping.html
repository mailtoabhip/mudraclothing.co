Where we ship

Anywhere in India we can get a courier to. That's most pin codes. If yours isn't covered, checkout will tell you before you pay anything.

We don't ship outside India yet.

What it costs

Nothing. Shipping is free on every order, whatever the size of it.

Printed to order

We don't sit on warehouses of stock. Each tee is printed after you order it, then packed and sent. That's why it takes a little longer than a shelf-pull, and why we rarely end up throwing unsold shirts away.

How long it takes

Printing & dispatch
3–5 working days from your order
Delivery
3–7 working days after dispatch, depending on your pin code
All in
Usually 6–12 working days door to door

Working days means Monday to Saturday, not counting public holidays. Metro cities are usually at the faster end. Remote pin codes, less so.

Cash on delivery

Available on orders across India. There's no extra charge for it. Please keep the exact amount ready. Couriers rarely have change.

If a COD order is refused at the door without a reason, we may ask for prepayment on your next order.

Tracking

When your order leaves us, you'll get an email and SMS with a tracking link. That link is the tracker. More on the track order page.

Delays and lost parcels

Couriers occasionally lose their way. If your order hasn't moved for a few days, or tracking says delivered and it wasn't, write to abhijeet.designmail@gmail.com with your order number. We'll chase it. If it's actually lost, we reprint and resend it at no cost, or refund you. Your call.

Wrong address

Check it at checkout. If you spot a mistake, email us straight away. We can fix it any time before dispatch. After that it's up to the courier.
