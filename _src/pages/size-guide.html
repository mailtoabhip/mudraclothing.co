HOW TO MEASURE

The quickest way to pick a size: take a tee you already like the fit of, lay it flat, and compare.

  1. A · ChestStraight across, armpit to armpit.
  2. B · LengthFrom the highest point of the shoulder, straight down to the hem.
  3. C · ShoulderSeam to seam across the back. On a drop shoulder, the seam sits past your actual shoulder.
  4. D · SleeveFrom the shoulder seam to the sleeve hem.
Fit

Boxy and relaxed through the body. The shoulder seam sits down the arm on purpose.
