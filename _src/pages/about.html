Before signatures, there were seals

Long before anyone in this country signed a name, they sealed it.

A merchant pressed his seal into wet clay, and a stranger at a faraway port knew whose goods these were. A king pressed his into wax, and a letter became a promise. The seal did all the talking. It said: this is mine, this is true, and I stand behind it. Then it said nothing more.

Mudraमुद्रा

The word for that seal is mudra. मुद्रा.

We took the word because we recognised the behaviour. One mark, pressed once, meaning everything. It is exactly how the quiet ones have always spoken.

So we made you one

Mudra is a seal for people who would rather be understood than explain themselves.

You put it on in the morning without a speech. You wear it to a chai stall, a lecture, a Friday at the office.
