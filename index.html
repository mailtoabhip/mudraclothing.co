Why Mudra

Before signatures,
there were seals.

In India, a seal said who you were without a word. Pressed into wax, clay or cloth, it meant this is mine, and I stand behind it. The old word for it is mudra.

We make t-shirts for people who work the same way. You don't announce yourself. You don't need a slogan across your chest. The right people notice anyway, the way you notice someone wearing the same song you've had on repeat all week.

Old word. New light. Rooted here, worn anywhere.

We make t-shirts. The rest is between you and your mirror.
